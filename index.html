Lihu Xu

I am a Professor in the University of Macau. My research interests lie in applied probability, statistics, and machine learning.

I work on probability approximation, stochastic differential equations, robust high-dimensional statistics, and sampling algorithms. I am particularly interested in mathematical questions motivated by modern statistical learning.

You can reach me at lihuxu@um.edu.mo.

Research

My current research includes:

  • Stein's method, Malliavin calculus, and probability approximation
  • Stochastic differential equations, SPDEs, ergodicity, and large deviations
  • Robust estimation and high-dimensional time series
  • Distribution estimation using deep neural networks
  • Langevin and Hamiltonian sampling methods
  • Stochastic algorithms in machine learning, including SGD and SVRG
