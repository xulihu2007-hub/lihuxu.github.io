I am a Professor at the University of Macau. In 2024, I was Visiting Faculty at Harvard University, and in 2026 I served as a tenured Associate Professor at Michigan State University.

My research spans applied probability, statistics, and machine learning. I work on Stein's method, Malliavin calculus, stochastic differential equations and SPDEs, ergodic theory, large deviations, stochastic algorithms, high-dimensional robust estimation, time series, neural distribution estimation, and Langevin and Hamiltonian sampling.
